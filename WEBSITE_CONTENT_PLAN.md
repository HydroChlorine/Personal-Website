# Lichen Huang personal website content plan

A public home on the internet for Lichen Huang / HydroChlorine: an introduction to who I am, things I make, ideas I enjoy, and what I am currently exploring. Chemistry, mathematics, and computer science are three connected parts of that identity. The site should remain useful and enjoyable as my interests and life change.

Graduate applications are one occasion for sharing the website. Faculty can discover my research and CV, while friends, fellow students, developers, and curious strangers can explore the same site through their own interests. The application documents provide background material; they do not set the website's structure or voice.

This document inventories content and relationships. It does not prescribe page layouts, visual styling, frontend components, or implementation. Names below are working labels. `[* …]` marks information or material to supply later; proposed articles and demonstrations are ideas, not claims that they already exist.

## 1. What the references suggest

- [Michael Xing's homepage](https://michaelxing.com/) introduces the person, includes interests beyond work, and invites exploration of projects, games, and writing. The useful principle here is a personal introduction with many paths into someone's work.

- [Michael's project collection](https://michaelxing.com/projects/) connects explanations and images with things visitors can use or play. Projects on this site should similarly offer something concrete to see, understand, or try.
- [dimden's homepage](https://dimden.dev/) combines projects and technical writing with personal updates, photos, links, and playful details. That mixture suggests room for both substantial work and everyday interests.
- Lichen specifically likes dimden's pixel art, animation, and animated weather atmosphere. Preserve those as preferences for a later visual brief. The content plan should support an expressive personal site without depending on a particular theme.
- These references guide the kind of experience being sought. Lichen's own interests, voice, content, and original assets should determine its identity.

## 2. Overall content structure

### Shared personal spaces

- **Home:** welcome, a small introduction, selected things to explore, and recent activity.
- **About:** who I am, personal background, interests, and a concise life/education/work history.
- **Projects:** a shared collection of things I have built, investigated, or made.
- **Writing / Notebook:** explanations, observations, project stories, and occasional personal posts.
- **Playground:** things visitors can run, manipulate, or play with, as material becomes available.
- **Contact / Elsewhere:** email and other places to find me, with access to a public CV.

### Three connected subject areas

- **Chemistry:** experiments, molecules, research, and the chemistry I find interesting.
- **Mathematics:** ideas, numerical experiments, explanations, and the mathematics I am learning.
- **Computer Science:** software, graphics, systems, proofs, and programming experiments.
- Each area should offer its own introduction and related projects, writing, and media.
- One item can belong to several areas. A numerical chemistry tool should have one main account of the work, linked from all relevant subjects.
- Personal material can sit outside these three subjects. The structure should accommodate interests that do not fit an academic label.
- The three areas have equal standing as parts of the site; their initial amounts of content can differ according to what is ready.
